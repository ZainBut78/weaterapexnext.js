## Things to know in September

- **Two national holidays:** Respect for the Aged Day (the third Monday of September) and Autumnal Equinox Day (around 22 or 23 September) are national holidays. In some years they create a run of days off known as "Silver Week", when travel gets busy.
- **Typhoons:** September is still typhoon season, and storms can disrupt flights and trains for a day or two.
- **Tokyo Game Show:** the Tokyo Game Show, a large video game exhibition, is usually held in September at Makuhari Messe in neighbouring Chiba.
- **Mount Fuji season ends:** the official Mount Fuji climbing season closes in early September.

## Getting around

The first half of September is usually still hot and humid, and the air starts to feel fresher later in the month. Check the weather forecast before day trips, especially if a typhoon is approaching.
