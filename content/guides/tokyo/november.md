## Things to know in November

- **Two national holidays:** Culture Day on 3 November and Labour Thanksgiving Day on 23 November are national holidays.
- **Shichi-Go-San:** around 15 November, families take children aged three, five and seven, often dressed in kimono, to shrines such as Meiji Jingu for the *Shichi-Go-San* celebration.
- **Autumn leaves:** autumn colours (*kōyō*) reach central Tokyo in late November and early December. The ginkgo tree avenue at Meiji Jingu Gaien turns bright yellow, and gardens such as Rikugien and Shinjuku Gyoen are popular for maple leaves.

## Getting around

November is usually dry and mild with cool evenings, which makes it one of the best months for walking. Popular gardens get crowded at weekends when the leaves are at their best, so weekday visits are calmer.
