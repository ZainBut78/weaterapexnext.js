## Things to know in December

- **Winter illuminations:** from November into December, many districts, such as Roppongi, Marunouchi and Shibuya, light up their streets with winter illuminations.
- **Christmas:** Christmas is not a public holiday in Japan and offices stay open, but the city is decorated and Christmas Eve is a popular evening to eat out, so restaurants get busy.
- **Year-end closures:** many offices, museums and some restaurants close from around the end of December until the first days of January. Check opening days before planning visits in the last week of the year.
- **New Year's Eve:** on the night of 31 December (*ōmisoka*), temples ring their bells 108 times (*joya no kane*) to see out the old year, and many people visit shrines and temples at midnight.

## Getting around

December is usually dry and sunny with cold evenings. Trains and highways get busy just before New Year as people travel home.
