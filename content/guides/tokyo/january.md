## Things to know in January

- **New Year holidays:** New Year (*shōgatsu*) is Japan's most important holiday. Many offices, some shops, restaurants and museums close for the first few days of January, while department stores reopen quickly with New Year sales.
- **First shrine visit:** in the first days of the year, millions of people make their first shrine or temple visit (*hatsumōde*). Meiji Jingu and Sensō-ji in Asakusa are extremely crowded.
- **Imperial New Year Greeting:** on 2 January the Emperor greets the public at the Imperial Palace, one of the few days the inner palace grounds are open.
- **Coming of Age Day:** the second Monday of January is a national holiday, when young adults in kimono visit shrines.

## Getting around

January is usually dry and sunny, with cold mornings. The observation decks of the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku are free and, on clear winter days, can offer views of Mount Fuji.
