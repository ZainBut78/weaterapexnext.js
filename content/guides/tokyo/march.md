## Things to know in March

- **Tokyo Marathon:** the Tokyo Marathon is usually held on the first Sunday of March. Roads through central Tokyo, from Shinjuku to Tokyo Station, are closed for much of the day.
- **Cherry blossom season begins:** the Japan Meteorological Agency announces when cherry blossoms open in Tokyo, usually in the second half of March. Full bloom usually follows about a week later, in late March or early April, and parks such as Ueno Park, Shinjuku Gyoen and the Chidorigafuchi moat become very crowded.
- **Vernal Equinox Day:** the spring equinox (around 20 or 21 March) is a national holiday.
- **School break:** the Japanese school year ends in March, so spring break brings more domestic travellers.

## Getting around

Hotels fill quickly during the cherry blossom period, so book early. Weather in March is changeable, with mild days and cold evenings.
