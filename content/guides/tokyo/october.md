## Things to know in October

- **Sports Day:** the second Monday of October is a national holiday.
- **Halloween in Shibuya:** Halloween is not a traditional Japanese holiday, but large crowds in costume gather around Shibuya Station in the evenings around 31 October. Shibuya City has asked people not to gather there and restricts drinking on the streets around the station during this period.
- **Comfortable weather:** after the summer heat and typhoons, October usually brings mild, drier days, one of the most comfortable times for walking around the city.

## Getting around

October is a popular month for day trips from Tokyo, and autumn leaves begin to change colour in the mountains near the city before they reach central Tokyo in November. Parks and gardens hold autumn events and flower displays.
