## Things to know in February

- **Setsubun:** on or around 3 February, temples and shrines hold *setsubun* ceremonies in which beans are thrown to drive away bad luck; Sensō-ji in Asakusa holds a well-known one.
- **Two national holidays:** National Foundation Day on 11 February and the Emperor's Birthday on 23 February are public holidays. On the Emperor's Birthday the Emperor usually greets the public at the Imperial Palace.
- **Plum blossoms:** plum (*ume*) trees flower from February into March, before the cherry blossoms. Yushima Tenjin shrine is a well-known place to see them.

## Getting around

February is one of the coldest months in Tokyo, but it is usually dry, with many clear days. It is a relatively quiet time for visitors between the New Year holidays and the spring cherry blossom season.
