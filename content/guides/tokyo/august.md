## Things to know in August

- **Obon:** around the middle of August, many people return to their family homes for Obon, a Buddhist holiday to honour ancestors. Long-distance trains, flights and highways are very busy, while central Tokyo can feel a little quieter, and some small shops close for a few days.
- **Mountain Day:** 11 August is a national holiday.
- **Typhoon season:** August and September are the main typhoon months. A typhoon can bring heavy rain and wind and disrupt flights and trains for a day or two.
- **Hot and humid:** August is usually the hottest month of the year in Tokyo.

## Getting around

Follow weather news and the Japan Meteorological Agency's warnings if a typhoon approaches. On hot days, the underground shopping streets and department stores are good places to escape the heat.
