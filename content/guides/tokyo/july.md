## Things to know in July

- **End of the rainy season:** the rainy season usually ends in mid- or late July, and then the hot, humid summer begins.
- **Sumida River Fireworks:** the Sumidagawa Fireworks Festival, one of Tokyo's oldest and largest firework displays, is held on the last Saturday of July near Asakusa. Streets and trains in the area are extremely crowded.
- **Marine Day:** the third Monday of July is a national holiday.
- **Mount Fuji season:** the official climbing season on Mount Fuji runs from early July to early September. Climbing is only recommended during this period.

## Getting around

Summer festivals (*matsuri*) and fireworks take place across the city. The heat and humidity can be intense, so plan indoor breaks, drink water, and carry a small towel, as many locals do.
