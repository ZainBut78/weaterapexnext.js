## Things to know in April

- **Cherry blossoms:** in many years Tokyo's cherry blossoms are still in full bloom in the first days of April, and petals fall soon after. Blossom-viewing picnics (*hanami*) fill parks at weekends.
- **New school and business year:** the Japanese school year and many companies' business year start on 1 April, so stations and trains are busy with students and new employees.
- **Golden Week begins:** Shōwa Day on 29 April is the first holiday of Golden Week, one of Japan's busiest travel periods. Trains, flights and hotels are heavily booked from the end of April.

## Getting around

April is one of the most pleasant and popular months to visit Tokyo. Book accommodation early, especially if your dates overlap with Golden Week, and expect queues at popular sights.
