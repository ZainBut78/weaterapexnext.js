## Things to know in June

- **Rainy season:** the rainy season (*tsuyu*) in the Tokyo area usually starts in early June and lasts until mid- or late July. The Japan Meteorological Agency announces its start and end each year. Days are often cloudy, humid and drizzly rather than stormy.
- **No national holidays:** June has no national holidays in Japan (December is the only other such month), so domestic travel is lighter than in May or August.
- **Hydrangeas:** hydrangeas (*ajisai*) flower during the rainy season, and gardens and temples known for them attract visitors.

## Getting around

Carry a compact umbrella; convenience stores everywhere sell umbrellas, and many museums, shopping streets and stations are covered or underground. Fewer tourists and no holiday rush can make June a calmer month for sightseeing.
