## Things to know in May

- **Golden Week:** Constitution Memorial Day (3 May), Greenery Day (4 May) and Children's Day (5 May) complete Golden Week. Many Japanese people travel, so trains, airports and popular sights are extremely busy in early May.
- **Sanja Matsuri:** one of Tokyo's biggest traditional festivals, the Sanja Matsuri of Asakusa Shrine, is held on the third weekend of May, with portable shrines (*mikoshi*) carried through the streets of Asakusa.
- **Children's Day streamers:** around Children's Day, carp-shaped streamers (*koinobori*) fly from homes and public places.

## Getting around

After Golden Week, the rest of May is usually warm and pleasant, with fewer crowds. It is a good month for walking, gardens and day trips before the rainy season starts in June.
