## Things to know in July

- **Opera at the Baths of Caracalla:** the Rome Opera House holds its summer season in the ruins of the ancient Baths of Caracalla, with open-air performances in the evenings.
- **Festa de' Noantri:** in July the Trastevere neighbourhood celebrates its traditional festival, with a religious procession and street events.
- **Summer sales:** Italy's summer sales start in early July.
- **Hot and busy:** July is one of the hottest and busiest months in Rome.

## Getting around

Start sightseeing early in the morning, rest in the shade or indoors in the afternoon, and go out again in the evening, when Romans do. Rome's public drinking fountains (*nasoni*) provide free drinking water all over the city, so carry a refillable bottle.
