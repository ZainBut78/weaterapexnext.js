## Things to know in May

- **Labour Day (1 May):** a national holiday. A large free concert (*Concerto del Primo Maggio*) is traditionally held in Piazza San Giovanni in Laterano.
- **Italian Open:** the Internazionali BNL d'Italia, one of the main clay-court tennis tournaments, is held at the Foro Italico in May.
- **Rose Garden:** the city's Rose Garden (*Roseto Comunale*) on the Aventine Hill, overlooking the Circus Maximus, opens to the public in spring, when the roses are in flower.

## Getting around

May is warm and sunny, ideal for walking, but it is also a busy month for tourists and school groups. Visit the most popular sites early in the day, and carry water as afternoons get warm.
