## Things to know in August

- **Ferragosto (15 August):** the Feast of the Assumption is a national holiday and the peak of the Italian summer holidays. Many Romans leave the city, and some small shops and restaurants close for part of August.
- **Snow in August:** on 5 August, the Basilica of Santa Maria Maggiore commemorates the legend of a miraculous summer snowfall, with white petals falling from the ceiling during the celebration.
- **Summer events:** the Caracalla opera season and outdoor summer events usually continue into August.

## Getting around

August is usually the hottest month. The main sights stay busy with visitors even though many residents are away. Check that the restaurant you plan to visit is open, and carry water — the free public drinking fountains are everywhere.
