## Things to know in November

- **All Saints' Day (1 November):** a national holiday, followed by All Souls' Day on 2 November, when many Romans visit cemeteries.
- **Rainiest time of year:** November is usually one of the wettest months in Rome, with shorter days.
- **Low season:** crowds are smaller than in spring and early autumn, which makes it easier to visit the Vatican Museums and the Colosseum.

## Getting around

On the first Sunday of every month, Italy's state museums and archaeological sites, including the Colosseum, the Roman Forum and the Palatine Hill, have free entry; expect long lines, and note that some sites need reservations. On rainy days, churches, the Pantheon and museums are good options. As the weather cools, roasted chestnut sellers appear on street corners in the historic centre.
