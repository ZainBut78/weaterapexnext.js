## Things to know in April

- **Easter:** Easter usually falls in April. On Easter Sunday the Pope celebrates Mass in St. Peter's Square and gives the *Urbi et Orbi* blessing. Easter Monday (*Pasquetta*) is a public holiday, when many Romans have picnics outside the city.
- **Rome's birthday (21 April):** the city celebrates its legendary founding with events and historical re-enactments, including a parade of people in ancient Roman costume.
- **Liberation Day (25 April):** a national holiday marking Italy's liberation at the end of the Second World War.

## Getting around

April is one of the most pleasant and busiest months in Rome. Book the Vatican Museums, the Colosseum and the Borghese Gallery in advance, especially around Easter and the April holidays.
