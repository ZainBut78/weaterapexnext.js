## Things to know in December

- **Immaculate Conception (8 December):** a national holiday. The Pope traditionally visits the column of the Virgin Mary near the Spanish Steps, and the city's Christmas season begins.
- **Christmas market:** the Christmas market in Piazza Navona usually runs from early December until Epiphany on 6 January.
- **Christmas at the Vatican:** a large nativity scene and Christmas tree are set up in St. Peter's Square, and the Pope celebrates Christmas Mass. Christmas Day and St. Stephen's Day (26 December) are public holidays.
- **New Year's Eve:** Romans see in the new year with fireworks and events across the city.

## Getting around

December is cool, with some rain, and days are short. Opening days of museums change around Christmas and New Year, so check before you go.
