## Things to know in June

- **Republic Day (2 June):** Italy's national day is a public holiday, marked by a military parade along Via dei Fori Imperiali, near the Colosseum. The area is closed and very crowded.
- **Saints Peter and Paul (29 June):** the feast of Rome's patron saints is a local public holiday in the city, with celebrations at St. Peter's Basilica.
- **Summer season:** from June, the city's summer programme brings open-air concerts, cinema and events to parks and historic sites.

## Getting around

June is warm to hot and one of the busiest months. Plan outdoor sightseeing for mornings and evenings, and use the midday heat for museums and churches. Remember that churches and the Vatican require covered shoulders and knees.
