## Things to know in October

- **Rome Film Festival:** the Rome Film Festival (*Festa del Cinema di Roma*) takes place in October, mainly at the Auditorium Parco della Musica, with premieres and public screenings.
- **Clocks go back:** summer time ends on the last Sunday of October, so it gets dark about an hour earlier.
- **Autumn weather:** October is usually mild, but rain becomes more frequent, especially later in the month, and some days bring heavy showers.

## Getting around

October is a pleasant month for walking, with comfortable temperatures and fewer crowds than in summer. Carry an umbrella, and plan museums such as the Capitoline Museums and the Vatican for rainy days. Italy's new-season wine (*vino novello*) arrives in shops and wine bars at the end of October.
