## Things to know in January

- **Epiphany:** 6 January is a public holiday in Italy. It is the day of the *Befana*, a kindly witch who brings sweets to children, and the Christmas market in Piazza Navona traditionally runs until Epiphany.
- **Winter sales:** Italy's winter sales (*saldi*) start in early January, and shopping streets such as Via del Corso are busy.
- **Quiet season:** after the holidays, January is one of the quietest months for visitors, so the Vatican Museums and major sites are less crowded than in spring.

## Getting around

January days are cool and often sunny. On the first Sunday of every month, Italy's state museums and archaeological sites, including the Colosseum, the Roman Forum and the Palatine Hill, have free entry; lines can be long, and some sites need reservations.
