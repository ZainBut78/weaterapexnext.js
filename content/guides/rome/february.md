## Things to know in February

- **Carnival:** Carnival (*Carnevale*) is celebrated in February or early March, depending on the date of Easter. In Rome it is mostly a family event, with children in costume and confetti in piazzas such as Piazza del Popolo and Piazza Navona.
- **Low season:** February is one of the quietest months for visitors, which makes it easier to book popular sites such as the Vatican Museums and the Borghese Gallery.
- **Papal audiences:** the Pope's weekly general audience usually takes place on Wednesdays, in St. Peter's Square or in the Paul VI Audience Hall in winter. Tickets are free but must be requested in advance.

## Getting around

February can be cool and damp, with some rainy days. Mornings in the Vatican and the Colosseum area are calmer than afternoons.
