## Things to know in March

- **Clocks go forward:** summer time starts on the last Sunday of March across the European Union, including Italy, so evenings become lighter.
- **Rome Marathon:** the Rome Marathon is usually held on a Sunday in March, passing many of the city's famous sites. Roads in the historic centre are closed for much of the day.
- **Easter:** in years when Easter falls in March, Holy Week brings large crowds to the Vatican for the Pope's services, and Easter Monday (*Pasquetta*) is a public holiday.

## Getting around

March is the start of spring. Days are mild and good for walking, but showers are common. It is still before the main tourist season, so queues are usually shorter than from April onwards.
