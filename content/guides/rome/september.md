## Things to know in September

- **Back to normal:** Romans return from their summer holidays, shops and restaurants that closed in August reopen, and schools start again in mid-September.
- **Still busy with visitors:** September is one of the most popular months to visit, with warm weather and slightly smaller crowds than in summer.
- **Heritage Days:** Italy takes part in the European Heritage Days in late September, when some state sites hold special openings and events.

## Getting around

The heat usually eases during September, making it a good month for long walks through the historic centre, the Appian Way and the city's parks. Book the Vatican Museums and the Colosseum in advance, as they remain busy. Early in the month the evenings are still long, which leaves time for walks at sunset along the Tiber or up to the Janiculum Hill.
