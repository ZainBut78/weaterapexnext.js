## Things to know in July

- **Wimbledon:** The Championships at Wimbledon, which start in late June, finish in July with the finals weekend. Tickets are hard to get; the public ballot and the daily queue are the main routes in.
- **The BBC Proms:** the BBC Proms, a classical music festival based at the Royal Albert Hall, begins in July and runs into September.
- **School holidays:** schools in England break up for the summer holidays in the second half of July, so family attractions get busier towards the end of the month.

## Getting around

July is peak season in London. Days are long, parks are busy, and popular sights have longer queues, so early starts help. On hot days the Underground can get very warm — carry water, especially on deep lines.
