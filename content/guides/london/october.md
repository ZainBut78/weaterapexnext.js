## Things to know in October

- **Clocks go back:** British Summer Time ends on the last Sunday of October, so from then it gets dark about an hour earlier in the evening.
- **School half-term:** many schools in England have a one-week half-term holiday in the second half of October, so family attractions can be busier that week.
- **Frieze London:** the Frieze London contemporary art fair is held in The Regent's Park in October. It is ticketed; Frieze Sculpture, an outdoor sculpture display in the park, can usually be seen for free.

## Getting around

Once the clocks go back, it gets dark in the late afternoon, so outdoor sightseeing works best earlier in the day. Autumn colours in the Royal Parks, such as Hyde Park, Regent's Park and Richmond Park, and at Kew Gardens are at their best around this time of year.
