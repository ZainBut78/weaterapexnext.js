## Things to know in February

- **Chinese New Year:** London's Chinese New Year celebrations take place in and around Chinatown and Trafalgar Square. Because the festival follows the lunar calendar, it falls in late January in some years and in February in others — check the date for your year.
- **School half-term:** schools in England have a one-week half-term holiday in mid-to-late February, so museums and family attractions can be busier that week.
- **London Fashion Week:** one of London's two fashion weeks each year is held in February (the other is in September). Most shows are trade events, but the area around venues gets busy.

## Getting around

February is rugby season: the Six Nations Championship is played in February and March, and England play their home matches at Twickenham Stadium in south-west London. Trains and pubs around Twickenham get very busy on match days. Away from match days, February is usually a quieter month for sightseeing than spring or summer.
