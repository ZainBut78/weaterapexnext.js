## Things to know in May

- **Two bank holidays:** England has an Early May bank holiday on the first Monday of May and a Spring bank holiday on the last Monday of May. Expect busier parks and attractions on those long weekends.
- **School half-term:** schools in England usually have a one-week half-term holiday at the end of May, around the Spring bank holiday.
- **RHS Chelsea Flower Show:** the Royal Horticultural Society's flower show is held in the grounds of the Royal Hospital Chelsea in May. It is ticketed and very popular, so tickets need to be booked early.

## Getting around

Long evenings and milder weather make May a good month for walking along the South Bank, visiting markets and spending time in parks. It is a popular time to visit, so booking accommodation ahead helps, especially around the bank holiday weekends.
