## Things to know in August

- **Notting Hill Carnival:** one of Europe's largest street festivals takes place in Notting Hill over the August bank holiday weekend (the Sunday and Monday). Some nearby Underground stations close or run exit-only, and the area is extremely crowded.
- **Summer bank holiday:** in England the Summer bank holiday is the last Monday of August.
- **School holidays:** schools in England are on summer holiday for the whole of August, so museums, zoos and family attractions are at their busiest.
- **The BBC Proms:** the Proms season at the Royal Albert Hall continues through August.

## Getting around

August is a busy, warm month, and booking timed entry for popular attractions helps avoid long queues. Summer evenings are the season for open-air performances: Regent's Park Open Air Theatre stages plays and musicals through the summer, and the Proms include late-evening concerts.
