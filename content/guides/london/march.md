## Things to know in March

- **Clocks go forward:** British Summer Time starts on the last Sunday of March. Clocks move forward one hour, so evenings suddenly feel much lighter.
- **St Patrick's Day:** London marks St Patrick's Day with a parade and a festival, usually on a weekend close to 17 March, organised with the Mayor of London.
- **The Boat Race:** the Oxford–Cambridge rowing races on the River Thames, between Putney and Mortlake, are held in late March or early April. The riverbanks are free to watch from and get crowded.
- **Easter:** in years when Easter falls in March, Good Friday and Easter Monday are bank holidays and many schools are on holiday.

## Getting around

March is the start of spring, but the weather is changeable — a light waterproof layer is useful. Royal Parks such as St James's Park and Hyde Park begin to show spring flowers this month.
