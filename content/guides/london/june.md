## Things to know in June

- **Trooping the Colour:** the King's Birthday Parade, known as Trooping the Colour, is held on a Saturday in June. Soldiers parade from Buckingham Palace along The Mall to Horse Guards Parade, and the day ends with an RAF flypast. The Mall is crowded and closed to traffic.
- **Wimbledon:** The Championships at Wimbledon, the grass-court tennis tournament, begin in late June and run into July.
- **Longest days:** the summer solstice falls in late June, so London has its longest days of the year this month.

## Getting around

June is one of the busiest months for visitors. Long, light evenings suit riverside walks and outdoor events, and many parks host open-air theatre and concerts in summer. Book popular attractions and accommodation ahead where you can.
