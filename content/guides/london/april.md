## Things to know in April

- **Easter holidays:** Easter usually falls in April. Good Friday and Easter Monday are bank holidays, and schools in England have a two-week Easter break around them, so attractions are busier during that period.
- **London Marathon:** the London Marathon is usually held on a Sunday in April. Roads across east and central London, including around Tower Bridge and the Embankment, are closed for most of the day.
- **The Boat Race:** in years when it is not held in late March, the Oxford–Cambridge Boat Race takes place in early April on the Thames.

## Getting around

Daylight is much longer than in winter now that British Summer Time has started. Spring blossom appears in the Royal Parks and at Kew Gardens. Weather can still switch between sunshine and showers within a day, so carry a light layer.
