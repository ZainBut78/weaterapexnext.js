## Things to know in December

- **Christmas season:** Hyde Park Winter Wonderland, with rides, markets and an ice rink, runs from late November into early January, and Christmas markets and lights appear across the city.
- **Christmas Day transport:** Transport for London does not run most services, including the Underground, on Christmas Day. Plan how you will get around on 25 December in advance.
- **Bank holidays:** Christmas Day and Boxing Day are bank holidays in England, and many shops and attractions close on Christmas Day.
- **New Year's Eve fireworks:** the Mayor of London's New Year's Eve fireworks are held on the River Thames by the London Eye. Viewing areas along the river are ticketed and sell out, so book well ahead.

## Getting around

December has the shortest days of the year in London. Opening hours of many attractions change over the Christmas period, so check before you go.
