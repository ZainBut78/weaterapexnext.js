## Things to know in January

- **New Year's Day Parade:** London holds a New Year's Day Parade through the West End every year on 1 January, with marching bands and performers. Streets along the route are closed, so plan central travel around it.
- **Bank holiday:** New Year's Day is a public holiday in England, so some shops and services keep holiday hours.
- **January sales:** many London shops run winter sales that start after Christmas and continue into January.
- **Quiet season:** after the holidays, January is one of the quieter months for visitors, so popular attractions are usually less crowded than in summer.

## Getting around

Days are short and often grey, so it helps to plan indoor stops. Many of London's major museums and galleries, such as the British Museum, the National Gallery and the Natural History Museum, have free general entry.
