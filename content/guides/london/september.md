## Things to know in September

- **Open House Festival:** each year, usually in September, many London buildings that are normally closed to the public open their doors for free tours as part of the Open House Festival, run by Open City. Some visits need booking in advance.
- **Totally Thames:** a month-long festival of events along the River Thames takes place in September.
- **London Fashion Week:** London's second fashion week of the year is held in September.
- **Last Night of the Proms:** the BBC Proms season ends in September with the Last Night at the Royal Albert Hall.

## Getting around

Schools in England return in early September, so after the first week attractions tend to be quieter than in August, while the weather is often still mild. That makes September a popular month for sightseeing on foot.
