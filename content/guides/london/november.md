## Things to know in November

- **Bonfire Night:** Guy Fawkes Night falls on 5 November. Firework displays are held in parks across London on that date and on nearby weekends; many are ticketed.
- **Lord Mayor's Show:** a large parade through the City of London takes place on the second Saturday of November to mark the new Lord Mayor of the City taking office.
- **Remembrance Sunday:** on the second Sunday of November a national service of remembrance is held at the Cenotaph in Whitehall. Roads around Whitehall are closed.
- **Christmas lights:** the Christmas lights on Oxford Street and Regent Street are usually switched on in November.

## Getting around

November brings short days and frequent rain, so indoor plans are useful. Free-entry museums, galleries and covered markets make good wet-weather options, and the city is generally quieter than in summer.
