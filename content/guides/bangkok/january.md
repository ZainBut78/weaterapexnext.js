## Things to know in January

- **Cool season:** January is in Bangkok's cool season (roughly mid-October to mid-February), the most comfortable time of year, with lower humidity and little rain. It is also peak tourist season.
- **New Year's Day** is a public holiday.
- **Chinese New Year:** in years when Chinese New Year falls in late January, Yaowarat Road in Chinatown is decorated with red lanterns and filled with food stalls and celebrations. In 2028 it falls in late January; in 2027 it is in early February.

## Getting around

The BTS Skytrain, the MRT and Chao Phraya river boats avoid Bangkok's heavy road traffic. The Grand Palace and major temples have a strict dress code: shoulders and knees must be covered, and visitors are asked to remove shoes inside temple halls.
