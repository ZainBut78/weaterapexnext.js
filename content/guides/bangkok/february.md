## Things to know in February

- **Chinese New Year (2027):** in 2027 Chinese New Year falls in early February. Yaowarat Road in Chinatown is at its busiest, with lanterns, lion dances and street food.
- **Makha Bucha:** Makha Bucha, an important Buddhist holiday, falls on the full moon of the third lunar month, usually in February or early March. It is a public holiday, and people visit temples for candlelit processions in the evening. Alcohol sales are restricted on major Buddhist holidays, with some exemptions that have changed in recent years, so check locally.
- **End of the cool season:** the weather warms up during the month as the cool season ends.

## Getting around

Dress respectfully if you join temple ceremonies. The BTS and MRT are the quickest way to reach Chinatown's nearby stations and avoid road traffic.
