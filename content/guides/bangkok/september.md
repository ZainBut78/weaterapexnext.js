## Things to know in September

- **Wettest month:** September is usually the wettest month of the year in Bangkok, with heavy afternoon and evening storms and occasional street flooding.
- **Vegetarian Festival:** during the first nine days of the ninth lunar month, usually in late September or October, Chinatown (Yaowarat) holds a vegetarian festival. Food stalls marked with yellow flags sell vegetarian and vegan dishes.
- **Low season:** tourist numbers are low, and major sights are less crowded.

## Getting around

Plan indoor attractions, such as museums, the Jim Thompson House and large malls, for the afternoons, and use the BTS Skytrain and MRT, which keep running during storms while roads get congested. River boats may be suspended in very bad weather.
