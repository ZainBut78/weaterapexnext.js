## Things to know in April

- **Songkran (13–15 April):** Songkran, the Thai New Year, is Thailand's biggest festival and a national holiday. Water fights take over streets such as Silom Road and Khao San Road, and people also visit temples and pour water over the hands of elders as a blessing. Expect to get wet if you go out, and protect your phone and documents.
- **Chakri Day (6 April):** a public holiday marking the founding of the current royal dynasty.
- **Hottest month:** April is usually the hottest month of the year in Bangkok.

## Getting around

Many residents travel home to other provinces for Songkran, so Bangkok's roads are quieter, but long-distance transport is very busy. Some shops and small businesses close for the holiday.
