## Things to know in August

- **Mother's Day (12 August):** the birthday of the late Queen Sirikit, the Queen Mother, is a public holiday and is celebrated as Mother's Day in Thailand, with decorations in blue and events in the city.
- **Rainy season:** August is one of the wetter months, with frequent afternoon and evening thunderstorms.
- **Low season:** fewer tourists visit during the rains, so major sights such as the Grand Palace and Wat Pho are usually less crowded than in the cool season.

## Getting around

Streets can flood briefly after heavy downpours, and traffic slows, so use the BTS Skytrain, the MRT and river boats where possible. Keep an umbrella or light rain jacket with you, and remember that temple dress rules (covered shoulders and knees) apply whatever the weather.
