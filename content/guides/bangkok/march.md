## Things to know in March

- **Hot season begins:** Bangkok's hot season runs roughly from mid-February to mid-May. March is hot and humid, with strong sun and very little rain.
- **Makha Bucha:** in some years the Buddhist holiday of Makha Bucha falls in early March. It is a public holiday, and alcohol sales are restricted, with some exemptions.
- **Kite season:** during the windy weeks of the hot season, people traditionally fly kites at Sanam Luang, the large field in front of the Grand Palace.

## Getting around

Visit temples and outdoor sights early in the morning, before the midday heat, and use air-conditioned malls, museums and the Skytrain in the afternoon. Chao Phraya river boats are a breezy way to reach riverside sights such as Wat Arun and the Grand Palace.
