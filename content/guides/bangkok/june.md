## Things to know in June

- **Queen's Birthday (3 June):** Queen Suthida's birthday is a public holiday.
- **Visakha Bucha:** in some years the Buddhist holiday of Visakha Bucha falls in early June rather than May.
- **Rainy season:** June is in the rainy season (roughly mid-May to mid-October). Rain usually comes as heavy afternoon or evening downpours rather than all-day rain, and mornings are often dry.
- **Low season:** with the rains, June is part of the low tourist season, so popular sights are less crowded.

## Getting around

Heavy downpours can flood some streets briefly and slow traffic, so the BTS Skytrain and MRT are more reliable than taxis in the afternoon. Keep an umbrella with you, and plan indoor attractions, such as museums and malls, for the rainiest part of the day.
