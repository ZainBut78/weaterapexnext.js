## Things to know in July

- **Asahna Bucha and Khao Phansa:** these two Buddhist holidays usually fall in July, on consecutive days around the full moon of the eighth lunar month. Khao Phansa marks the start of the three-month Buddhist Lent, when monks stay in their temples. Temples hold candle processions, and alcohol sales are restricted, with some exemptions.
- **King's Birthday (28 July):** King Vajiralongkorn's birthday is a public holiday, and buildings are decorated with portraits and yellow flags.
- **Rainy season:** July brings frequent afternoon and evening showers.

## Getting around

Rain usually comes in heavy but short bursts, so plan sightseeing for the morning. Buddhist Lent is also a traditional time for young men to be ordained as monks for a short period, so you may see ordination ceremonies at temples. Dress respectfully when visiting temples during the religious holidays.
