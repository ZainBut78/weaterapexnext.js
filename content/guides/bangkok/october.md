## Things to know in October

- **Two memorial holidays:** 13 October marks the passing of King Bhumibol (Rama IX), and 23 October is Chulalongkorn Day, honouring King Chulalongkorn (Rama V). Both are public holidays.
- **Ok Phansa:** the end of Buddhist Lent (*Ok Phansa*) falls on the full moon of the eleventh lunar month, usually in October. Temples hold ceremonies, and alcohol sales are restricted, with some exemptions.
- **Vegetarian Festival:** in some years Chinatown's vegetarian festival falls in October.
- **End of the rains:** the rainy season usually ends around mid-October, although the first half of the month can still be wet.

## Getting around

As the weather becomes drier and a little cooler towards the end of the month, outdoor sightseeing along the river and in the old town becomes more comfortable.
