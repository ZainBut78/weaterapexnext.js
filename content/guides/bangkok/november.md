## Things to know in November

- **Loy Krathong:** Loy Krathong is celebrated on the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month, usually in November. In the evening people float small decorated baskets (*krathong*) with candles and flowers on the Chao Phraya River, canals and lakes, such as those in Lumphini and Benchakitti parks. It is not a public holiday, but riverside areas are very crowded.
- **Cool season begins:** the cool season, roughly mid-October to mid-February, brings lower humidity and little rain, and the busy tourist season starts.

## Getting around

November is one of the most comfortable months for walking the old town, riverside temples and markets. River boats get very busy on Loy Krathong evening, so plan your route to and from the riverside in advance. Book accommodation early for the high season.
