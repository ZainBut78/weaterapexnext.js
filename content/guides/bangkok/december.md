## Things to know in December

- **Father's Day (5 December):** the birthday of the late King Bhumibol (Rama IX) is a public holiday, also celebrated as Father's Day and National Day, with decorations in yellow across the city.
- **Constitution Day (10 December):** a public holiday.
- **New Year's Eve:** 31 December is also a public holiday. Countdown events and fireworks are held along the Chao Phraya River and at large malls, and the riverside is very crowded.
- **Peak season:** December is one of the busiest and most pleasant months to visit, with cooler, dry weather.

## Getting around

Book accommodation early for the holiday period. The BTS Skytrain and MRT are the easiest way to reach the countdown areas, as roads near the river and the main malls close or become congested on New Year's Eve.
