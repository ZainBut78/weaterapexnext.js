## Things to know in May

- **Labour Day and Coronation Day:** 1 May (Labour Day) and 4 May (Coronation Day) are public holidays.
- **Visakha Bucha:** Visakha Bucha, the most important Buddhist holiday, marks the birth, enlightenment and death of the Buddha. It falls on the full moon of the sixth lunar month, usually in May or early June. It is a public holiday with temple ceremonies, and alcohol sales are restricted, with some exemptions.
- **Royal Ploughing Ceremony:** an ancient ceremony at Sanam Luang marks the start of the rice-growing season, usually in May.
- **Rainy season begins:** the rainy season usually starts around mid-May.

## Getting around

May is hot and humid, with more afternoon thunderstorms as the rains begin. Carry an umbrella, and plan outdoor sightseeing for the morning.
