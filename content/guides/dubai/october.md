## Things to know in October

- **Start of the season:** October marks the start of Dubai's busy season. Days are still hot, but evenings become more comfortable, and outdoor venues begin to reopen.
- **Global Village opens:** the Global Village cultural and shopping park usually opens its new season in October.
- **Dubai Fitness Challenge:** the 30-day Dubai Fitness Challenge usually starts at the very end of October or on 1 November and runs through November, with free fitness events across the city.

## Getting around

Outdoor dining and evening walks along the Creek, the beaches and Dubai Marina become pleasant again, though midday is still hot enough to plan indoor breaks. The Metro is the easiest way to reach Downtown Dubai and the Marina.
