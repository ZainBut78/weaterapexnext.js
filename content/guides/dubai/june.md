## Things to know in June

- **Summer heat:** from June to September, daytime outdoor sightseeing in Dubai is hard because of the heat. Most visitors plan their days around indoor attractions and go outside early in the morning or after sunset.
- **Islamic New Year:** the Islamic (Hijri) New Year is a public holiday. Its date moves about 11 days earlier each year; in 2027 it is expected in early June and in 2028 in late May.
- **Summer offers:** summer is the low season for tourism, so many hotels and attractions run summer promotions, and the Dubai Summer Surprises festival usually starts around the end of June or in July.

## Getting around

Use the Metro, taxis and covered walkways rather than walking long distances outdoors. Indoor options include large malls with ice rinks and aquariums, and an indoor ski slope at Mall of the Emirates.
