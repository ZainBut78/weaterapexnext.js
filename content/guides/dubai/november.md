## Things to know in November

- **Dubai Fitness Challenge:** the Dubai Fitness Challenge usually runs through November, encouraging residents and visitors to exercise for 30 minutes a day for 30 days. Its best-known events include a mass run and a mass cycle on Sheikh Zayed Road, which is closed to cars for them.
- **Dubai Airshow (odd years):** the Dubai Airshow, one of the world's largest aerospace shows, is held in November in odd-numbered years at Dubai World Central, near Al Maktoum International Airport.
- **Comfortable weather:** November brings warm, pleasant days and cooler evenings, and outdoor attractions are fully open.

## Getting around

With comfortable weather, the beaches, the souks and desert trips are all popular. Road closures during Dubai Fitness Challenge events and the Airshow can affect traffic, so check before travelling.
