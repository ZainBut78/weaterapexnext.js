## Things to know in February

- **Ramadan (2027 and 2028):** the dates of Ramadan move about 11 days earlier each year. In 2027 Ramadan is expected to start in early February, and in 2028 in late January, so in both years most of February falls in Ramadan. Exact dates depend on the moon sighting.
- **During Ramadan:** Muslims fast from dawn to sunset. Visitors are not expected to fast, but out of respect it is best not to eat, drink or smoke in public places during fasting hours. Many restaurants, malls and attractions stay open, working hours are shorter, and evenings are lively with iftar meals after sunset.
- **Outdoor season:** February is still in the cooler, comfortable part of the year, and Global Village and outdoor venues are open.

## Getting around

Opening hours of shops, attractions and some Metro services can change during Ramadan, so check before you go.
