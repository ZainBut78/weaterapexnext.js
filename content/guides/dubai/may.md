## Things to know in May

- **Eid al-Adha (2027 and 2028):** Eid al-Adha, together with Arafat Day, is a public holiday of several days in the UAE. Its date moves about 11 days earlier each year; in 2027 it is expected in mid-May and in 2028 in early May. Exact dates depend on the moon sighting.
- **End of the outdoor season:** Global Village usually closes for the summer around May, and many outdoor venues and beach clubs shorten their programmes as the heat builds.
- **Hotter days:** May is hot, and temperatures keep rising through the month.

## Getting around

Air-conditioned attractions make the heat manageable: Dubai has large indoor malls, aquariums and museums. Beaches are best early in the morning or in the late afternoon, and water and sun protection are essential.
