## Things to know in August

- **Heat and humidity:** August is one of the hottest and most humid months of the year. Outdoor sightseeing is only comfortable very early in the morning, and humidity makes evenings feel sticky.
- **Dubai Summer Surprises:** the summer festival of sales and family events usually continues through August.
- **Prophet's Birthday:** the Prophet Muhammad's birthday is a public holiday whose date moves about 11 days earlier each year; in 2027 it is expected in mid-August and in 2028 in early August.
- **Back to school:** schools in the UAE usually reopen at the end of August, and residents return from summer travel.

## Getting around

This is the low season, so hotel demand and crowds at attractions are usually at their lowest of the year. Stick to indoor attractions during the day and use the Metro and taxis rather than walking.
