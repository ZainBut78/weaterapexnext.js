## Things to know in March

- **Eid al-Fitr (2027):** Eid al-Fitr, the festival at the end of Ramadan, is a public holiday of several days. In 2027 it is expected in early March; in 2028 it falls in late February instead. Exact dates depend on the moon sighting, and hotels and attractions are busy over the holiday.
- **Dubai World Cup:** the Dubai World Cup, one of the world's richest horse races, is usually held at Meydan Racecourse in late March or early April.
- **Warming up:** March is still pleasant, but days get noticeably warmer towards the end of the month, which makes it one of the last comfortable months for long outdoor sightseeing.

## Getting around

Beaches, parks and outdoor markets are at their best early in the month. During Eid, roads to malls and attractions can be congested in the evenings, so the Metro is often quicker.
