## Things to know in April

- **Getting hot:** April marks the end of Dubai's comfortable season. Days are already hot, and by the end of the month the midday sun makes long walks outdoors tiring.
- **Dubai World Cup:** in years when it is not held in late March, the Dubai World Cup horse race takes place at Meydan Racecourse in early April.
- **Global Village:** the Global Village season usually continues into April and ends around May, so April is one of the last chances to visit in a given season.

## Getting around

Plan outdoor sightseeing, such as the Creek, the souks and the beaches, for mornings and evenings, and use the middle of the day for air-conditioned places like malls and museums. The Metro and air-conditioned bus stops make it easy to get around without long walks in the sun.
