## Things to know in July

- **Dubai Summer Surprises:** the Dubai Summer Surprises festival usually runs through July and August, with sales in malls, family entertainment and hotel offers.
- **School holidays:** schools in the UAE are on their summer break in July and August, and many residents travel abroad, so the city itself is quieter while malls are busy with families.
- **Hottest time of year:** July is one of the hottest months. Outdoor sightseeing in the middle of the day is not practical, and even evenings stay very warm.

## Getting around

Plan the day around air-conditioned places: malls, museums, aquariums, indoor theme parks and the indoor ski slope at Mall of the Emirates. If you go to the beach, go early in the morning, and carry water whenever you are outside.
