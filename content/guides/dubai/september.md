## Things to know in September

- **Still hot:** September is still very hot and humid in Dubai, especially in the first half of the month. The heat usually starts to ease slightly towards the end of the month, but outdoor daytime sightseeing remains hard.
- **Schools are back:** the new school year has started, so the city and roads are busier again after the summer.
- **Season preparations:** many outdoor venues, markets and attractions that closed for the summer prepare to reopen for the cooler season from October.

## Getting around

September is still low season, which can mean quieter attractions and summer offers at hotels. Beaches and outdoor areas are best at sunrise or after sunset, and indoor attractions such as malls, museums and aquariums are the main daytime options.
