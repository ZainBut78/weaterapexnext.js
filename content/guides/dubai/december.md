## Things to know in December

- **National Day:** UAE National Day is on 2 December and Commemoration Day on 1 December. They are usually observed together as a public holiday, with fireworks, flags and celebrations across Dubai.
- **Dubai Shopping Festival:** the Dubai Shopping Festival usually begins in December and runs into January, with sales, fireworks and concerts.
- **New Year's Eve:** Dubai's New Year's Eve fireworks at the Burj Khalifa in Downtown Dubai draw very large crowds. Many viewing areas need tickets or reservations, and roads in Downtown close early in the evening.
- **Peak season:** December is one of the busiest times to visit, and hotels book up early, especially around the holidays.
- **GITEX Global:** GITEX Global, a large technology exhibition, moved to December at Expo City Dubai in 2026; check the official dates, as hotels fill up that week.

## Getting around

Book hotels early for the holiday period. On New Year's Eve, Metro stations near Downtown close or run with changes, so plan your route in advance.
