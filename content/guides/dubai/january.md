## Things to know in January

- **Peak season:** January is one of the most popular months to visit Dubai, with warm days that are comfortable for being outdoors. Hotels and beaches are busy, so booking ahead helps.
- **Dubai Shopping Festival:** the Dubai Shopping Festival usually runs from December into January, with sales across malls and markets, fireworks and live events.
- **Global Village:** the Global Village cultural and shopping park is open for its season, which usually runs from around October to May.
- **Weekend:** since 2022 the UAE government weekend has been Saturday and Sunday, with Friday a shorter working day for government offices. Many private companies follow a similar pattern.
- **New Year's Day** is a public holiday.

## Getting around

The Dubai Metro and taxis are the easiest ways to reach the malls, the beaches and Global Village. Evenings can feel cool outdoors, so bring a light layer.
