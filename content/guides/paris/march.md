## Things to know in March

- **Clocks go forward:** summer time starts on the last Sunday of March across the European Union, including France. Clocks move forward one hour, so evenings become noticeably lighter.
- **Winter school holidays end:** in some years the Zone C (Paris) winter school holidays continue into early March, so family attractions can stay busy at the start of the month.
- **Fashion Week:** the womenswear shows of Paris Fashion Week, which begin in late February, finish in early March.
- **Easter:** in years when Easter falls in March, Easter Monday is a public holiday in France.

## Getting around

March is the start of spring, but the weather is changeable and showers are common, so a light waterproof layer is useful. The city's parks and gardens start to turn green, and it is still a quieter month for visitors than April to June.
