## Things to know in April

- **Easter Monday:** Easter usually falls in April, and Easter Monday is a public holiday in France.
- **Spring school holidays:** French schools have two weeks of spring holidays in April, staggered by zone (Paris is in Zone C), so museums and attractions get busier during those weeks.
- **Paris Marathon:** the Paris Marathon is usually held on a Sunday in April, starting and finishing near the Champs-Élysées and the Avenue Foch area. Many central roads are closed on race day.
- **Foire de Paris:** the Foire de Paris, a large consumer fair for home, food and crafts, opens at Paris Expo Porte de Versailles in late April and continues into May.

## Getting around

April is the start of the busy season. Blossom appears in parks and gardens, and outdoor café terraces fill on sunny days. The weather can still change quickly between sun and showers, so carry a light layer.
