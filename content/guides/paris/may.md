## Things to know in May

- **Many public holidays:** May has several public holidays in France: Labour Day on 1 May, Victory in Europe Day on 8 May, and Ascension Day (a Thursday, in May or early June). Many Parisians take long weekends around them.
- **1 May:** on Labour Day it is a tradition to give sprigs of lily of the valley (*muguet*), sold on street corners. Several major museums, including the Louvre, are closed on 1 May.
- **European Night of Museums:** on a Saturday in May, museums across France open in the evening for the *Nuit européenne des musées*, with free entry.
- **Roland-Garros:** the French Open tennis tournament at Roland-Garros, in the west of Paris, begins in late May and runs into June.

## Getting around

Long, mild days make May one of the most pleasant months for walking. It is also a busy month, and the long weekends fill hotels and trains, so book ahead.
