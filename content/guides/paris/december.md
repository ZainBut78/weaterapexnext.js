## Things to know in December

- **Christmas windows:** the big department stores on Boulevard Haussmann, Galeries Lafayette and Printemps, decorate their windows with animated Christmas displays, which draw crowds, especially at weekends.
- **Christmas lights:** the Champs-Élysées and many other streets are lit up for the festive season, from late November into early January.
- **School holidays:** French schools have two weeks of Christmas holidays, the same for all zones.
- **Christmas Day:** 25 December is a public holiday, and several major museums, including the Louvre, are closed that day.
- **New Year's Eve:** crowds gather on the Champs-Élysées to see in the new year, usually with a light show on the Arc de Triomphe.

## Getting around

December has the shortest days of the year in Paris. Opening days of attractions change over Christmas and New Year, so check before you go.
