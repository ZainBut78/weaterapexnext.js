## Things to know in August

- **Holiday month for Parisians:** August is the traditional summer holiday month in France. Many Parisians leave the city, and some small shops, bakeries and restaurants close for their annual break (*fermeture annuelle*), usually with a notice on the door.
- **Assumption Day:** 15 August is a public holiday in France.
- **Paris Plages:** the summer riverside beaches along the Seine and at the Bassin de la Villette continue into August.
- **Rock en Seine:** the Rock en Seine music festival takes place in late August in the Domaine national de Saint-Cloud, just west of Paris.

## Getting around

With many residents away, parts of the city feel quieter, while the main sights stay busy with visitors. If you have a favourite neighbourhood restaurant in mind, check that it is open before you go.
