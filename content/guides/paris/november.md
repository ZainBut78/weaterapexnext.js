## Things to know in November

- **Two public holidays:** All Saints' Day on 1 November and Armistice Day on 11 November are public holidays in France. On 11 November a national ceremony is held at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier under the Arc de Triomphe, and the area around it is closed.
- **Beaujolais Nouveau:** the new Beaujolais wine is released on the third Thursday of November, and many Paris bars and wine shops mark the day.
- **Christmas lights:** the Christmas illuminations on the Champs-Élysées are usually switched on in late November.

## Getting around

November is one of the greyest and wettest months, with short days, so indoor plans are useful. The permanent collections of the City of Paris museums, such as the Petit Palais and the Musée Carnavalet, are free to visit, and covered passages (*passages couverts*) are pleasant to explore on rainy days.
