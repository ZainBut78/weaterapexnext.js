## Things to know in September

- **European Heritage Days:** on the third weekend of September, the *Journées européennes du patrimoine* open buildings that are usually closed to the public, including government buildings and ministries, often for free. Popular sites have long queues or need booking.
- **La rentrée:** early September is *la rentrée*, when schools reopen and Parisians return from their summer holidays. Shops and restaurants that closed in August reopen, and the city gets busy again.
- **Fashion Week:** the spring-summer womenswear shows of Paris Fashion Week begin in late September and continue into early October.

## Getting around

After the August lull, September combines a lively city with weather that is often still mild, which makes it a popular month for walking tours and café terraces. Hotels can be busy around Fashion Week and trade fairs.
