## Things to know in June

- **Fête de la Musique:** every year on 21 June, France celebrates the Fête de la Musique. Free concerts take place on streets, squares and in bars all over Paris, and the city is lively late into the night.
- **Roland-Garros:** the French Open finishes in early June with the finals at Roland-Garros.
- **Paris Pride:** the Paris Pride march (*Marche des Fiertés*) usually takes place on a Saturday in late June.
- **Whit Monday:** Whit Monday (*lundi de Pentecôte*), in May or June depending on the year, is a public holiday.
- **Longest days:** the summer solstice falls in late June, so Paris has its longest days of the year.

## Getting around

June is one of the busiest months for visitors. Long, light evenings suit picnics along the Seine and in parks. Book popular museums and monuments with timed entry where possible.
