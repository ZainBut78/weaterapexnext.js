## Things to know in January

- **Public holiday:** New Year's Day is a public holiday in France, and several major museums, including the Louvre, are closed on 1 January.
- **Winter sales:** France's winter sales (*les soldes*) start in January. Their start date is set nationally each year and they last four weeks, so shops across Paris are busy, especially at weekends.
- **Galette des rois:** in January bakeries across Paris sell the *galette des rois*, a puff-pastry cake eaten for Epiphany, with a small figurine hidden inside.
- **Quiet season:** after the holidays, January is one of the quieter months for visitors, so big sights are usually less crowded than in spring or summer.

## Getting around

Days are short and often grey, so indoor plans help. The permanent collections of the City of Paris museums, such as the Petit Palais and the Musée Carnavalet, are free to visit.
