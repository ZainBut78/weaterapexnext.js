## Things to know in July

- **Bastille Day:** 14 July is France's national holiday. In the morning a military parade goes down the Champs-Élysées, and in the evening there is a large fireworks display at the Eiffel Tower. Central streets are closed and very crowded.
- **Tour de France:** the Tour de France usually finishes in Paris in late July, with the final stage in the city centre.
- **Paris Plages:** each summer the City of Paris sets up *Paris Plages*, with deckchairs and activities along the banks of the Seine and at the Bassin de la Villette.
- **Summer sales:** France's summer sales start in late June or early July and last four weeks.
- **School holidays:** French schools are on summer holiday from early July.

## Getting around

July is peak season: expect queues at major sights, so early starts and timed tickets help. On hot days, parks and the Paris Plages areas are good places to cool down.
