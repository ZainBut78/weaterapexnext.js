## Things to know in February

- **Winter school holidays:** French schools have two weeks of winter holidays in February and early March. The dates are staggered by zone, and Paris is in Zone C, so check which weeks apply to Paris in your year.
- **Chinese New Year:** Paris celebrates Chinese New Year with parades, especially in the 13th arrondissement. Because it follows the lunar calendar, it falls in late January in some years and in February in others.
- **Paris International Agricultural Show:** the *Salon International de l'Agriculture*, a large farming and food fair at Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, runs from late February into early March.
- **Fashion Week:** the autumn-winter womenswear shows of Paris Fashion Week take place in late February and early March.

## Getting around

February is still winter in Paris, with cold, often damp days. The agricultural show and the school holidays bring extra crowds, so booking popular attractions ahead helps.
