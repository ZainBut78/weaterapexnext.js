## Things to know in October

- **Clocks go back:** summer time ends on the last Sunday of October across the European Union, so from then it gets dark about an hour earlier in the evening.
- **All Saints' school holidays:** French schools have a two-week autumn break (*vacances de la Toussaint*) from the second half of October into early November, the same for all zones.
- **Salon du Chocolat:** the Salon du Chocolat, a large chocolate fair at Paris Expo Porte de Versailles, is held in late October or early November.
- **Fashion Week:** the spring-summer womenswear shows of Paris Fashion Week finish in early October.

## Getting around

October brings shorter, cooler days and more rain than summer. Autumn colours in parks and gardens are at their best, and after the clocks go back, outdoor sightseeing works best earlier in the day.
