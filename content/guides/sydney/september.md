## Things to know in September

- **Spring begins:** in Australia spring officially starts on 1 September. Gardens and parks come into flower, and the weather is usually mild and sunny.
- **Spring school holidays:** New South Wales schools usually have a two-week break from late September into early October.
- **Football finals:** late September and early October are the finals season for Australia's main football codes. The rugby league (NRL) grand final is usually played in Sydney, when the city is busy with fans.

## Getting around

September is a pleasant month for walking the harbour foreshore, the Royal Botanic Garden and coastal paths. The sea is still cool for swimming. Whale-watching trips continue as whales head back south.
