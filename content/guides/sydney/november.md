## Things to know in November

- **Jacaranda season:** jacaranda trees are in full purple bloom across Sydney in November, with well-known displays in Kirribilli and around the University of Sydney.
- **Sculpture by the Sea:** in years when it is held, the coastal sculpture exhibition between Bondi and Tamarama continues into early November.
- **Beach season:** beaches get busier as the weather warms, and lifeguard patrols are in place on the main beaches.

## Getting around

November is late spring, usually warm and sunny, with long evenings thanks to daylight saving time. The UV index is already high, so use sunscreen and a hat even on mild days, and swim between the red and yellow flags. Afternoon thunderstorms can occur on hot days. Schools are still in session, so weekdays at the beaches are quieter than in December and January.
