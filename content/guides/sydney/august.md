## Things to know in August

- **City2Surf:** the City2Surf fun run, from the city centre to Bondi Beach, is usually held on the second Sunday of August and is one of the largest running events in the world. Roads along the route are closed that morning.
- **End of winter:** August is usually dry, with cool mornings and increasingly mild afternoons.
- **Whale watching:** humpback whales can still be seen along the coast.

## Getting around

August is one of the driest months of the year in Sydney, which makes it good for coastal walks and outdoor sightseeing. Wattle trees start to flower in yellow in bushland around the city. Evenings are still cool, so bring a jacket. With the winter school holidays over, museums and attractions are quieter on weekdays.
