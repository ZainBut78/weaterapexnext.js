## Things to know in January

- **Summer holidays:** January is the middle of summer and of the long school holidays in New South Wales, so beaches, ferries and attractions are busy.
- **Sydney Festival:** the Sydney Festival brings theatre, music, dance and free outdoor events to the city in January.
- **Australia Day (26 January):** a national public holiday, with events around Sydney Harbour and in parks across the city.

## Getting around

January is hot and sunny, and the UV index is very high, so use strong sunscreen, a hat and shade even on cloudy days. At the beach, swim between the red and yellow flags, which mark areas patrolled by lifeguards. Ferries from Circular Quay are an easy way to see the harbour.
