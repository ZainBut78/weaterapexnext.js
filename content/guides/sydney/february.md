## Things to know in February

- **Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras:** the Mardi Gras festival runs through February, ending with its famous night-time parade along Oxford Street, usually on a Saturday in late February or early March.
- **Lunar New Year:** Sydney celebrates Lunar New Year with a festival in January or February, depending on the year, with events around Chinatown and Haymarket.
- **Schools return:** the school year in New South Wales starts in late January or early February, so family attractions become quieter on weekdays.

## Getting around

February is still summer, usually warm and humid, with occasional heavy thunderstorms. Beaches remain busy at weekends. Streets around Oxford Street are closed and crowded on the night of the Mardi Gras parade.
