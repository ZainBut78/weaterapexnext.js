## Things to know in June

- **Winter begins:** in Australia winter officially starts on 1 June. Sydney's winter is mild, with cool, often sunny days and cold nights.
- **Vivid Sydney:** the Vivid light festival usually continues into mid-June.
- **King's Birthday:** the second Monday of June is a public holiday in New South Wales.
- **Sydney Film Festival:** the Sydney Film Festival is held in June, with screenings at the State Theatre and other cinemas.

## Getting around

June has the shortest days of the year in Sydney. Beaches are quiet, but coastal walks and harbour ferries are pleasant on clear days. Whale-watching season continues along the coast. June and July are the busiest weeks of the humpback whales' northward migration.
