## Things to know in December

- **Summer begins:** in Australia summer officially starts on 1 December, and the long school summer holidays begin in the second half of December.
- **Christmas:** Christmas Day and Boxing Day are public holidays. Many people spend Christmas at the beach or in parks, and Bondi Beach is especially busy on Christmas Day.
- **Sydney to Hobart:** on Boxing Day (26 December) the Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race starts from Sydney Harbour, and crowds watch from the foreshore.
- **New Year's Eve:** the fireworks over Sydney Harbour Bridge and the Opera House are among the most famous in the world. Many foreshore viewing areas are ticketed or fill up early in the day.

## Getting around

December is warm to hot with a very high UV index. Book accommodation early for Christmas and New Year, and plan transport home after the fireworks.
