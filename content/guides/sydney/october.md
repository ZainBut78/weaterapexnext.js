## Things to know in October

- **Clocks go forward:** daylight saving time in New South Wales starts on the first Sunday of October, giving long, light evenings.
- **Labour Day:** the first Monday of October is a public holiday in New South Wales.
- **Sculpture by the Sea:** in most years, from mid- or late October into early November, the free outdoor exhibition Sculpture by the Sea lines the coastal walk from Bondi to Tamarama with large artworks.
- **Jacarandas:** purple jacaranda trees start to flower across Sydney in late October.

## Getting around

October is warm and sunny, with the beach season beginning. The Bondi to Tamarama walk gets very crowded during Sculpture by the Sea, especially at weekends, so weekday mornings are calmer.
