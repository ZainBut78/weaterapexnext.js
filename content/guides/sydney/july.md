## Things to know in July

- **Middle of winter:** July is usually the coolest month in Sydney. Days are mild and often sunny, but nights can be cold, and many homes and older buildings are not heavily heated.
- **School holidays:** New South Wales schools have a two-week winter break in July, so museums and family attractions are busier.
- **Whale watching:** July is a good month to see humpback whales migrating north along the coast.

## Getting around

Pack a warm jacket for the evenings. Winter is a good time for indoor attractions such as the Art Gallery of New South Wales and the Australian Museum, and for walks in the Royal Botanic Garden on sunny days. The Blue Mountains are a popular day trip, but they are much colder than the city.
