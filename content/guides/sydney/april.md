## Things to know in April

- **Clocks go back:** daylight saving time in New South Wales ends on the first Sunday of April, so evenings get darker earlier.
- **Easter:** Easter usually falls in April. Good Friday, Easter Saturday, Easter Sunday and Easter Monday are public holidays in New South Wales, and school holidays often fall around Easter.
- **Anzac Day (25 April):** a national day of remembrance. A dawn service is held at the Cenotaph in Martin Place, followed by a march through the city centre.
- **Royal Easter Show:** the show at Sydney Olympic Park often continues into April.

## Getting around

April brings mild autumn weather, pleasant for walking around the harbour and the city. Opening hours change over the Easter weekend, so check before you go.
