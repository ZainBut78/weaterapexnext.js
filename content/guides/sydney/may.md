## Things to know in May

- **Vivid Sydney:** the Vivid Sydney festival of light, music and ideas usually begins in late May and runs into June. Buildings including the Sydney Opera House are lit up at night, and the harbour area is very crowded in the evenings.
- **Whale season begins:** humpback whales migrate along the New South Wales coast from around May to November, and whale-watching cruises leave from Sydney Harbour.
- **Late autumn:** May brings cooler, drier days and crisp evenings.

## Getting around

Public transport is the best way to reach the Vivid light installations, as roads around Circular Quay are busy and parking is limited. Coastal headlands, such as those near Bondi and North Head, are good places to look for whales from land.
