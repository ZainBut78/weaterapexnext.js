## Things to know in March

- **Autumn begins:** in Australia autumn officially starts on 1 March. Days are still warm, and the sea is at its warmest after the summer.
- **Mardi Gras parade:** in some years the Mardi Gras parade on Oxford Street is held in early March.
- **Sydney Royal Easter Show:** the Royal Easter Show, a large agricultural show with rides and animals at Sydney Olympic Park, is held around Easter, which falls in March or April.

## Getting around

March is often a good time for the beaches, with warm water and smaller crowds than in January. Rain becomes more frequent, and some days bring heavy showers, so check the forecast before coastal walks such as Bondi to Coogee.
