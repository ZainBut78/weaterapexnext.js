## Things to know in January

- **Three Kings:** on the evening of 5 January the Three Kings Parade (*Cavalcada de Reis*) goes through the city, and 6 January (Epiphany) is a public holiday, when children traditionally receive their presents.
- **Winter sales:** the winter sales (*rebaixes*) start in early January, and shopping streets such as Passeig de Gràcia and Portal de l'Àngel are very busy.
- **Quiet season:** after the holidays, January is one of the calmest months for visitors, with shorter queues at popular sights.

## Getting around

Days are mild but short, and evenings are cool. Barcelona's municipal museums are free on the first Sunday of every month, and many also on Sunday afternoons, which makes a good plan for a grey winter day.
