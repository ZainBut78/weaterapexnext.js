## Things to know in October

- **Clocks go back:** summer time ends on the last Sunday of October across the European Union, so it gets dark about an hour earlier in the evening.
- **Hispanic Day:** 12 October is a national public holiday in Spain.
- **Autumn weather:** October is usually still mild, but it is also one of the rainier months, and heavy showers can arrive quickly.
- **Castanyada:** at the end of the month, chestnut stalls appear on the streets ahead of All Saints' Day, when Catalans traditionally eat roasted chestnuts, sweet potatoes and small almond cakes called *panellets*.

## Getting around

With fewer tourists than in summer and comfortable temperatures, October suits long walks through the Gothic Quarter and up to Montjuïc. Carry an umbrella in case of a sudden shower.
