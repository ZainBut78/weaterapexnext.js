## Things to know in November

- **All Saints' Day:** 1 November is a public holiday, and the *castanyada* tradition of roasted chestnuts and *panellets* is celebrated around it.
- **Christmas lights:** the city's Christmas lights are usually switched on in late November.
- **Christmas markets begin:** the Fira de Santa Llúcia, Barcelona's traditional Christmas market in front of the Cathedral, usually opens at the end of November.
- **Low season:** November is one of the quietest months for visitors, so popular sights are easier to book.

## Getting around

November brings cooler, shorter days and some rain. Barcelona's municipal museums are free on the first Sunday of every month, and many also on Sunday afternoons — useful on a wet day.
