## Things to know in April

- **Sant Jordi (23 April):** Catalonia's Saint George's Day is one of Barcelona's most loved days. Streets such as La Rambla and Passeig de Gràcia fill with stalls selling books and roses, and people traditionally exchange them. It is not a public holiday, so shops stay open.
- **Easter:** Easter usually falls in April. Good Friday and Easter Monday are public holidays in Catalonia, and Holy Week is a busy travel period.
- **Spring season:** April is the start of the busy season, with mild, pleasant days.

## Getting around

Book popular sights such as the Sagrada Família and Park Güell ahead, as timed tickets sell out around Easter. Showers are still possible, so bring a light layer.
