## Things to know in August

- **Festa Major de Gràcia:** in mid-August the Gràcia neighbourhood holds its week-long festival, when residents decorate whole streets with handmade themes, and there are concerts and events every night.
- **Assumption Day:** 15 August is a public holiday.
- **Holiday month:** many locals take their holidays in August, and some small shops and restaurants close for part of the month, while beaches and the main sights are at their busiest.
- **Festa Major de Sants:** the neighbouring district of Sants holds its own street festival in late August.

## Getting around

August is hot and humid. The Gràcia festival gets very crowded in the evenings, so visit the decorated streets earlier in the day if you prefer to see them calmly.
