## Things to know in September

- **La Diada (11 September):** the National Day of Catalonia is a public holiday, often marked by large gatherings and marches in the city centre.
- **La Mercè:** Barcelona's biggest festival honours the city's patron saint around 24 September, which is a local public holiday. For several days there are free concerts, human towers (*castells*), giants and a fire run (*correfoc*) with fireworks.
- **Back to routine:** schools reopen in September, and the city returns to normal life after the August break.

## Getting around

September is often still warm enough for the beach, with fewer crowds than in August. During La Mercè, some streets close for events, and the Metro is the easiest way to move around.
