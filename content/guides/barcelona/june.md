## Things to know in June

- **Sant Joan:** the night of 23 June (*Revetlla de Sant Joan*) is one of the liveliest nights of the year, with fireworks, bonfires and parties across the city and on the beaches. 24 June is a public holiday in Catalonia.
- **Summer season starts:** schools break up in late June, beaches get crowded, and outdoor concerts and events begin.
- **Primavera Sound:** in some years the Primavera Sound festival continues into early June.

## Getting around

June is warm and sunny, with the longest days of the year. On the night of Sant Joan, public transport runs with special services, and beaches are extremely busy, so plan how you will get home. Book popular attractions ahead.
