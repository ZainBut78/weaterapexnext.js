## Things to know in July

- **Grec Festival:** the Grec Festival, Barcelona's summer festival of theatre, dance and music, takes place in July, with performances at the open-air Teatre Grec on Montjuïc and other venues.
- **Peak season:** July is one of the busiest months, with crowded beaches, long queues at the main sights and high demand for accommodation.
- **Summer sales:** the summer sales start in early July.

## Getting around

July is hot and humid, so plan sightseeing for the morning and evening, and take breaks in the shade or indoors in the middle of the day. Book timed tickets for popular sights such as the Sagrada Família and Park Güell well ahead, and keep an eye on your belongings in crowded areas.
