## Things to know in March

- **Clocks go forward:** summer time starts on the last Sunday of March across the European Union, including Spain, so evenings become noticeably lighter.
- **Barcelona Marathon:** the Barcelona Marathon is usually held on a Sunday in March, and roads along the route are closed for much of the morning.
- **Easter:** in years when Easter falls in March, Good Friday and Easter Monday are public holidays in Catalonia, and Holy Week brings extra visitors.
- **Mobile World Congress:** in some years the Mobile World Congress takes place in early March rather than late February.

## Getting around

March is the start of spring. Days are mild and good for walking, though showers are possible, and the beaches are still quiet. Outdoor café terraces start to fill on sunny afternoons.
