## Things to know in February

- **Santa Eulàlia festival:** around 12 February the city celebrates Santa Eulàlia, one of Barcelona's patron saints, with a winter festival of giants (*gegants*), human towers (*castells*) and events in the Old Town.
- **Carnival:** Barcelona's Carnival (*Carnestoltes*) takes place in February or early March, depending on the date of Easter, with parades in many neighbourhoods. The nearby town of Sitges holds one of Spain's best-known carnivals.
- **Mobile World Congress:** the Mobile World Congress, a very large technology fair at Fira Gran Via, is usually held in late February or early March. Hotels fill up and become hard to book during that week.

## Getting around

February is usually mild and fairly dry. Check the Mobile World Congress dates before booking, as the whole city is busier that week.
