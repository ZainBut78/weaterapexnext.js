## Things to know in December

- **Fira de Santa Llúcia:** the traditional Christmas market in front of the Cathedral runs until just before Christmas, selling nativity figures, decorations and the Catalan *caga tió*, a log figure that children "feed" and then hit to make it "give" sweets on Christmas Eve.
- **Public holidays:** Constitution Day (6 December) and the Immaculate Conception (8 December) are national holidays, often creating a long weekend. Christmas Day and Sant Esteve (26 December) are both holidays in Catalonia.
- **New Year's Eve:** people gather in public spaces to see in the new year, traditionally eating twelve grapes at midnight.

## Getting around

December is mild compared with northern Europe, but days are short. Many shops close on 25 and 26 December, so plan meals ahead.
