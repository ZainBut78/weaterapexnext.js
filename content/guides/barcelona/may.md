## Things to know in May

- **Labour Day:** 1 May is a public holiday, and some shops close.
- **Whit Monday:** Whit Monday (*Segona Pasqua*), in May or June depending on the date of Easter, is a local public holiday in Barcelona.
- **Primavera Sound:** the Primavera Sound music festival is held at the Parc del Fòrum, usually in late May or early June, and attracts large international crowds.
- **Beach season begins:** the city's beach services usually start in spring, and the sea is still cool in May.

## Getting around

May is one of the most comfortable months for walking, with warm days and long evenings. It is also busy, so book accommodation early, especially around festival dates.
