## Things to know in January

- **Holidays:** New Year's Day is a federal holiday, and Martin Luther King Jr. Day falls on the third Monday of January. Some offices and services close, while most shops and attractions stay open.
- **Quiet season:** after the holiday rush, January is one of the quieter months for visitors, so big attractions and Broadway theatres are usually less crowded than in December.
- **Winter weather:** January is one of the coldest months in New York, and snowstorms can affect flights and street travel for a day or two.

## Getting around

Warm indoor stops are useful on cold days. The Staten Island Ferry is free and gives a view of the Statue of Liberty and the Lower Manhattan skyline, and the Smithsonian's National Museum of the American Indian in Lower Manhattan has free entry.
