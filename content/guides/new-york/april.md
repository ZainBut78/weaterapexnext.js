## Things to know in April

- **Easter Parade:** on Easter Sunday — usually in April, sometimes in late March — the Easter Parade and Bonnet Festival takes place on Fifth Avenue around St. Patrick's Cathedral, with people showing off decorated hats. It is informal and free to watch.
- **Spring recess:** New York City public schools have a spring recess, usually in April around Passover and Easter, so family attractions are busier that week.
- **Spring blossoms:** cherry and magnolia trees flower in Central Park and at the Brooklyn Botanic Garden in April.

## Getting around

April brings milder weather and longer days, but rain showers are common, so carry a light waterproof layer. It is a popular month for visitors, so book hotels and Broadway tickets ahead, especially around the school holidays.
