## Things to know in September

- **Labor Day and the Carnival:** Labor Day, the first Monday of September, is a federal holiday. On that day the West Indian American Day Carnival parade takes place along Eastern Parkway in Brooklyn, one of the city's largest street events.
- **US Open finals:** the US Open tennis championships in Queens finish in the first half of September.
- **Feast of San Gennaro:** one of the city's oldest street festivals, held since 1926, the Feast of San Gennaro fills Mulberry Street in Little Italy for 11 days, usually in mid-September, with food stalls, music and a religious procession.
- **UN General Assembly:** in late September world leaders come to the United Nations headquarters for the General Assembly's high-level week. Security closures cause heavy traffic in Midtown East.
- **Fashion Week:** New York Fashion Week returns in September.

## Getting around

Schools reopen after Labor Day and the summer heat usually eases during the month, which makes September a good time for walking. During UN week, the subway is usually quicker than taxis for crossing Midtown.
