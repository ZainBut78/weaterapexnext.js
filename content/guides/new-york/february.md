## Things to know in February

- **Lunar New Year:** Manhattan's Chinatown celebrates Lunar New Year with a firecracker ceremony and a parade. Because the festival follows the lunar calendar, it falls in late January in some years and in February in others. Lunar New Year is also a New York City public school holiday.
- **Presidents' Day:** the third Monday of February is a federal holiday.
- **Midwinter recess:** New York City public schools have a week-long midwinter recess in February, so museums and family attractions get busier that week.
- **Fashion Week:** New York Fashion Week is held in February and again in September.

## Getting around

February is still deep winter, with cold days and a chance of snow. It is often a good month for indoor plans such as museums, Broadway shows and food halls, and hotel demand is usually lower than in spring or December.
