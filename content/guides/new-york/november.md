## Things to know in November

- **New York City Marathon:** the marathon is held on the first Sunday of November. It passes through all five boroughs and finishes in Central Park, and many roads and bridges are closed that day.
- **Clocks go back:** daylight saving time in the United States ends on the first Sunday of November — about a week later than in Europe.
- **Veterans Day:** 11 November is a federal holiday, marked with a parade on Fifth Avenue.
- **Thanksgiving:** on the fourth Thursday of November, the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, with its giant balloons, goes through Manhattan. Crowds line the route from early morning.

## Getting around

The Thanksgiving parade balloons are inflated the day before, on the streets around the American Museum of Natural History, and many people go to watch. The parade route closes streets on the Upper West Side and in Midtown on Thanksgiving morning.
