## Things to know in March

- **Clocks go forward:** daylight saving time in the United States starts on the second Sunday of March — earlier than in Europe. For a few weeks the time difference between New York and Europe is one hour smaller than usual.
- **St. Patrick's Day Parade:** New York's St. Patrick's Day Parade marches up Fifth Avenue on 17 March (on the day before when 17 March is a Sunday). It is one of the oldest and largest parades in the city, and Midtown is crowded all day.
- **Spring break:** many US colleges have spring break in March, which brings extra visitors to the city.

## Getting around

March weather swings between late-winter cold and the first mild days, so pack layers. Parade routes close streets in Midtown, so check subway options if you are crossing Fifth Avenue on parade day.
