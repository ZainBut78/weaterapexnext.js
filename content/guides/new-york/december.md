## Things to know in December

- **Rockefeller Center Christmas Tree:** the tree at Rockefeller Center is lit in a ceremony shortly after Thanksgiving and stays up through the holiday season. The plaza and the nearby ice rink are among the busiest spots in Midtown.
- **Holiday windows:** department stores on Fifth Avenue decorate their windows for the holidays, drawing crowds especially in the evenings and at weekends.
- **Christmas Day:** 25 December is a federal holiday, and many shops and some attractions are closed.
- **New Year's Eve in Times Square:** the ball drop in Times Square draws very large crowds. Viewing areas are free but fill up hours before midnight, and there are strict security checks.

## Getting around

December is the busiest time of year in Midtown Manhattan. The subway runs 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays, and is usually quicker than taxis at this time of year.
