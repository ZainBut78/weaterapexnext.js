## Things to know in June

- **NYC Pride March:** the NYC Pride March takes place on the last Sunday of June, passing through Greenwich Village, near the Stonewall National Monument. It is one of the largest Pride events in the world, and streets along the route are closed.
- **Juneteenth:** 19 June is Juneteenth, a federal holiday.
- **Tribeca Festival:** the Tribeca Festival of film, music and talks is held in Lower Manhattan in June.
- **Free summer events:** from June, the city's parks host free outdoor concerts, films and performances, including free Shakespeare in Central Park staged by The Public Theater.

## Getting around

Long, warm days make June a busy month. Humidity starts to rise, so plan walking in the morning or evening. Schools in New York City finish for the summer in late June.
