## Things to know in May

- **Memorial Day:** the last Monday of May is Memorial Day, a federal holiday that marks the unofficial start of summer. Parks and beaches get busy over the long weekend.
- **Fleet Week:** during Fleet Week New York, held around Memorial Day, US Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard ships dock in the city and some are open for free public visits.
- **Five Boro Bike Tour:** in early May a large, organised bike ride passes through all five boroughs, and many streets and bridges on the route are closed to traffic that morning.

## Getting around

May is one of the most pleasant months in New York, with mild temperatures and long evenings, which makes it good for walking the High Line, Central Park and the Brooklyn waterfront. It is also a popular month, so book accommodation ahead.
