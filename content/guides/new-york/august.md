## Things to know in August

- **Summer Streets:** on several summer days, usually including Saturdays in August, the city closes selected streets to cars for Summer Streets, so people can walk, run and cycle along them.
- **US Open:** the US Open tennis championships begin in late August at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows, Queens, and run into September.
- **Hot and humid:** August is usually hot and humid, with occasional heavy thunderstorms that can briefly flood streets and delay subway services.

## Getting around

Many New Yorkers take summer holidays in August, but the main sights stay busy with visitors. Early starts help at popular attractions. The subway is the quickest way to reach Flushing Meadows for the US Open.
