## Things to know in July

- **Independence Day:** 4 July is a federal holiday. Macy's 4th of July Fireworks is the city's largest display; the viewing location along the rivers changes from year to year, and prime viewing areas fill up early.
- **Summer in the parks:** free outdoor concerts, films and performances continue in parks across the five boroughs.
- **Peak summer:** July is one of the hottest and most humid months. Heat waves happen most summers, and the city opens cooling centres during heat emergencies.

## Getting around

Subway platforms can be very hot in summer while trains are air-conditioned, so carry water. Plan outdoor sightseeing for the morning or evening, and use museums and other air-conditioned spaces for the hottest part of the day.
