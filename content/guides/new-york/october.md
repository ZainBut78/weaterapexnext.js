## Things to know in October

- **Village Halloween Parade:** on 31 October the Village Halloween Parade goes up Sixth Avenue in Greenwich Village. Anyone in costume can join the parade, and the streets around it are very crowded.
- **Open House New York:** during the Open House New York weekend in October, many buildings and sites that are usually closed to the public open for tours. Some visits need booking in advance.
- **Autumn colours:** leaves start to change colour in the city's parks in October, and the colours in Central Park are usually at their best from late October into November.

## Getting around

October is often mild and dry, one of the most comfortable months for exploring on foot. Evenings get cooler, so bring a jacket for after dark.
