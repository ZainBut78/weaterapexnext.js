## Things to know in February

- **Chinese New Year (2027):** in 2027 Chinese New Year falls in early February. The first two days are public holidays, and some shops and small restaurants, especially family-run ones, close for a few days. Celebrations include light-ups in Chinatown, the River Hongbao festival at Marina Bay and the Chingay Parade.
- **Thaipusam:** in some years the Hindu festival of Thaipusam falls in February, with a procession from Little India to Tank Road.
- **Ramadan and Hari Raya Puasa:** Ramadan dates move about 11 days earlier each year. In 2028 Hari Raya Puasa, the festival at the end of Ramadan, falls in late February and is a public holiday.

## Getting around

February is often one of the drier, breezier months of the Northeast Monsoon. Public transport runs as normal during festive holidays.
