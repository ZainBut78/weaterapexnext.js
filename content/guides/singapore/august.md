## Things to know in August

- **National Day (9 August):** Singapore's national day is a public holiday. The National Day Parade, with fireworks and aerial displays, is held in the evening; tickets are allocated by ballot, but the fireworks can be seen from many places around Marina Bay. Flags decorate the city throughout August.
- **Hungry Ghost Festival:** during the seventh month of the lunar calendar, usually in August or September, some Chinese communities make offerings to ancestors and spirits, and *getai* (live stage shows) are held in neighbourhoods.
- **Southwest Monsoon:** the drier Southwest Monsoon continues, with occasional early-morning squalls.

## Getting around

Roads around Marina Bay close on National Day and on rehearsal evenings, so use the MRT. Watch where you step during the Hungry Ghost Festival, as offerings are often placed along roadsides.
