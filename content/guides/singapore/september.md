## Things to know in September

- **Singapore Grand Prix:** the Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix, a night race on the Marina Bay Street Circuit, is usually held in September or early October. Roads around Marina Bay and the Civic District close for several days, and hotel demand rises sharply that week.
- **Mid-Autumn Festival:** the Mid-Autumn Festival takes place in September or early October, depending on the lunar calendar. Chinatown and Gardens by the Bay display lanterns, and mooncakes are sold everywhere.
- **Haze:** in some years regional smoke haze can affect air quality around this time.

## Getting around

Check the Grand Prix dates before booking, as the city centre is busy and some attractions change their access routes. Evenings are the best time to see the lantern displays.
