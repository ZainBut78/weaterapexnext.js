## Things to know in July

- **National Day rehearsals:** on several Saturdays before National Day, full rehearsals of the National Day Parade take place, often with fireworks and aerial displays that can be seen from around Marina Bay.
- **Southwest Monsoon:** July is usually one of the drier months, although early-morning squalls can bring short bursts of heavy rain and strong winds.
- **National flags:** ahead of National Day on 9 August, Singapore flags and decorations go up on buildings and streets across the city during July.

## Getting around

The Marina Bay waterfront gets crowded on rehearsal evenings, and some roads close. The MRT is the easiest way to get there. Outdoor sightseeing is most comfortable in the early morning or after sunset. July is part of the school term, so weekday attractions are usually calmer than in June.
