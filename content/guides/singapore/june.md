## Things to know in June

- **Southwest Monsoon:** from June to September, Singapore is in the Southwest Monsoon season. It is usually drier than the end of the year, but early-morning squalls — lines of thunderstorms with strong winds — can pass over the island.
- **School holidays:** Singapore schools have a mid-year break in June, so attractions such as Sentosa and the zoo are busier.
- **Haze:** in some years, smoke from land and forest fires in the region causes haze between about June and October. The National Environment Agency publishes air-quality readings.

## Getting around

June is hot and humid. Check haze readings if you plan long outdoor activities. Indoor attractions and malls connected to MRT stations make it easy to avoid the midday heat. Gardens by the Bay and the Botanic Gardens are most pleasant in the early morning, before the heat builds.
