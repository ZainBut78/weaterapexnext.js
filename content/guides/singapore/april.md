## Things to know in April

- **Inter-monsoon:** April is in the inter-monsoon period, which brings some of the hottest days of the year, light winds and frequent afternoon or evening thunderstorms.
- **Good Friday:** in years when Easter falls in April, Good Friday is a public holiday in April.
- **Fewer big holidays:** April has fewer festivals than the months around it, so it can be a calmer time to visit attractions.

## Getting around

Lightning is common during afternoon storms, so follow any closures at outdoor attractions, pools and beaches. Covered walkways link many MRT stations to nearby buildings, which helps on rainy days. Air-conditioned museums, such as the National Gallery Singapore and the ArtScience Museum, are good options during the hottest part of the day.
