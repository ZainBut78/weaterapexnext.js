## Things to know in October

- **Deepavali (2027 and 2028):** Deepavali, the Hindu Festival of Lights, is a public holiday. Its date follows the Hindu calendar; in 2027 it falls in late October and in 2028 it is expected in mid-October. In the weeks before, Little India is decorated with street lights and festive bazaars.
- **Inter-monsoon:** October and November are an inter-monsoon period, with frequent afternoon and evening thunderstorms.
- **Singapore Grand Prix:** in some years the night race is held in early October.

## Getting around

Little India is very crowded in the evenings before Deepavali, especially at weekends; the MRT is the easiest way to get there. Carry an umbrella for afternoon storms. Storms are usually heavy but short, and many attractions are indoors or under cover.
