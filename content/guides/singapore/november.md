## Things to know in November

- **Christmas light-up:** Orchard Road is decorated with Christmas lights from around mid-November into early January, one of the city's best-known festive displays.
- **Inter-monsoon and wetter weather:** November is usually one of the wettest months, with frequent thunderstorms as the inter-monsoon period gives way to the Northeast Monsoon.
- **Deepavali:** in some years Deepavali falls in early November instead of October.

## Getting around

Keep an umbrella handy and plan indoor options for stormy afternoons. The Singapore Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, have free entry to the main gardens, and many malls along Orchard Road are linked by underground passages to MRT stations. The year-end school holidays begin in late November, so family attractions get busier towards the end of the month.
