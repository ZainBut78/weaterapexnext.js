## Things to know in January

- **Northeast Monsoon:** from December to early March, Singapore is in the Northeast Monsoon season. December and January are usually the wettest months, with longer spells of rain, while late January and February are often drier and windier.
- **Chinese New Year (2028):** Chinese New Year follows the lunar calendar. In 2028 it falls in late January; in 2027 it is in early February. Chinatown is decorated with lights and festive markets in the weeks before.
- **Thaipusam:** Thaipusam, a Hindu festival, takes place in January or February. Devotees walk in procession between two temples, from Little India to Tank Road.

## Getting around

Singapore is warm and humid all year. Carry an umbrella for sudden showers. The Botanic Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, have free entry to the main gardens.
