## Things to know in March

- **Hari Raya Puasa (2027):** in 2027 Hari Raya Puasa, which marks the end of Ramadan, falls in March and is a public holiday. During Ramadan, the Geylang Serai area hosts a large evening bazaar with food and festive goods.
- **Good Friday:** Good Friday is a public holiday in Singapore; in 2027 it falls in late March.
- **Inter-monsoon begins:** after the Northeast Monsoon ends in early March, the inter-monsoon period brings hot afternoons and frequent thunderstorms, often in the afternoon or evening.

## Getting around

Showers are usually heavy but short. Plan outdoor visits, such as Gardens by the Bay and the Southern Ridges, for the morning, and keep indoor options for stormy afternoons.
