## Things to know in May

- **Labour Day:** 1 May is a public holiday.
- **Hari Raya Haji and Vesak Day (2027):** in 2027 both fall in mid-May, only a few days apart. Hari Raya Haji (Eid al-Adha) is a Muslim festival, and Vesak Day marks the birth, enlightenment and passing of the Buddha, with temple ceremonies and candlelight processions. In 2028 both are also expected in May. The exact dates move every year.
- **Hot and humid:** May is one of the hottest months, and the inter-monsoon period brings afternoon thunderstorms.

## Getting around

With several public holidays close together, attractions and malls can be busy, and many residents travel abroad. Temples welcome visitors on Vesak Day, but dress modestly and follow the temple's guidance.
