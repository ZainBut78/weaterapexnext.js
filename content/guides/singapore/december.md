## Things to know in December

- **Northeast Monsoon:** the Northeast Monsoon starts in December. December and January are usually the wettest months, with some days of long, steady rain.
- **School holidays:** Singapore schools are on their year-end holidays from late November through December, so attractions are busier.
- **Christmas:** Christmas Day is a public holiday. Orchard Road and Marina Bay are decorated with lights, and Gardens by the Bay usually hosts a Christmas event.
- **New Year's Eve:** the countdown at Marina Bay ends with fireworks over the bay, and the waterfront gets very crowded.

## Getting around

Plan indoor activities for rainy days and book popular attractions ahead during the holiday season. On New Year's Eve, MRT services run later than usual, and some roads around Marina Bay close.
